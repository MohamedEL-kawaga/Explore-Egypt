Welcome to Egypt

Egypt is one of the world's oldest civilizations and one of the most fascinating travel destinations. From the ancient pyramids of Giza to the beautiful beaches of the Red Sea, Egypt offers something for every traveler.

Egypt is located in northeastern Africa and is home to the Nile River, the longest river in the world

Taste the Soul of Egypt

Discover the rich flavors, traditional recipes, and unforgettable dishes that tell the story of Egypt.Discover the rich flavors, traditional recipes, and unforgettable dishes that tell the story of Egypt.

Koshari
01

EA beloved Egyptian dish made with rice, lentils, pasta, chickpeas, and crispy onions, topped with rich tomato sauce.

Ful Medames
02

A traditional Egyptian dish made with slow-cooked fava beans, olive oil, lemon, and warm spices, served with fresh bread.

Hamam Mahshi
03

A classic Egyptian delicacy of roasted pigeon stuffed with seasoned rice, herbs, and spices, offering a rich traditional flavor.

Mahshi
04

A popular Egyptian dish of vegetables and vine leaves stuffed with seasoned rice, fresh herbs, and aromatic traditional spices.

Molokhia
05

A famous Egyptian dish made from finely chopped jute leaves, cooked with garlic and coriander, and served with rice.
